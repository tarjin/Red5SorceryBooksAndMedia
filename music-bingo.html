✷ Featured Edition · 13 July 1985

Live Aid 1985

Wembley + JFK · 100 songs · 2,000 cards · 10 playlists

On 13 July 1985, two stadiums an ocean apart — Wembley in London and JFK in Philadelphia — staged the largest concert ever attempted: sixteen hours of music, watched by nearly two billion people, to feed a famine. Every song in this deck was performed live on one of those two stages that day — Queen's era-defining set, U2's “Bad,” the Led Zeppelin reunion, Bowie, Madonna, Dire Straits, the Who, Elton John, Black Sabbath, and dozens more. No later hits, no filler — just the music of the day itself. At 100 songs across eight 50-song games and two 75-song Marathons, it's a double pack — 2,000 cards and ten playlists, twice the size of a standard edition.

$5 from every purchase goes to the Souls Harbour Rescue Mission in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada — about one hot meal for a neighbour in need. Donations are made at the end of each month, based on that month's sales. Feeding people was the whole point in 1985; on this pack, it still is.
