Music Bingo

Unless otherwise stated, every edition ships with eight hundred printable bingo cards across four 50-song playlists, plus a 75-song marathon playlist with 200 more cards for a longer night — a thousand cards in all, built for players, hosts, and venues. Every square is numbered, so the table can call it out — "32!" — and tell each other exactly which song to look for. Every pack also includes a CSV song list — artist and title for each track — so you can easily build the playlists yourself in Spotify or any third-party tool. Built for the Room

Players · hosts · venues
For the players A night, not a worksheet

Big, readable cards that hold up under bar lighting — bold song title, artist underneath. Every square is numbered, so the table can call a number across the room and help each other keep up. Fast, social, and friendly a couple of pints in.

For the hosts A system, not a download

Curated, chart-authoritative themes for recurring nights — 800 unique cards per edition, plus a bonus 75-song Marathon playlist with 200 more cards for the long nights, and a host guide that keeps things moving and finishing on time. The playlists and cards arrive built and ready — you just play the songs from the library you already use. No theme to research, nothing to assemble: hours of prep off your plate every week, and a polished, ready-to-run night you can walk into a new venue and pitch with confidence.

For the venues A calendar, not a coin toss

A recurring event that draws a crowd and a tab — and a host who never scrambles for next week's theme. With a deep catalogue on the shelf, themes get locked in weeks ahead, so the venue can post the schedule, run the social push, and build buzz before the doors open. Clean cards with no logo on the play surface, printed to match whoever walks in.

✷ Venue-tested in live play

Proven in the Room

The Canadian Signatures edition made its live debut on Friday, 19 June 2026 — the one chance to bring it to the Legion floor before Canada Day — at the Somme Branch Legion in Dartmouth, Nova Scotia, hosted by Brandon “The Babe” Entertainment — a working DJ and bingo host across the HRM — running his own night to one of the bigger crowds the room had seen. Brandon ran the pack's bonus 75-song Marathon playlist, the long-night option, and a hundred printed cards barely covered the floor. It ran clean all the way to a finish on 69 songs called, ended on three tied full-card winners, and wrapped right on time. From the mic, he thanked Red5Sorcery by name — then asked for custom theme packs for future nights. One player took two cards home to show people. Brandon had billed it to his own crowd ahead of the night as ‘an early Canada Day Playlist’ from Red5Sorcery — fitting, for an all-Canadian deck landing on a Legion floor just days before the holiday.

✷ Featured Edition · 13 July 1985

Live Aid 1985

Wembley + JFK · 100 songs · 2,000 cards · 10 playlists

On 13 July 1985, two stadiums an ocean apart — Wembley in London and JFK in Philadelphia — staged the largest concert ever attempted: sixteen hours of music, watched by nearly two billion people, to feed a famine. Every song in this deck was performed live on one of those two stages that day — Queen's era-defining set, U2's “Bad,” the Led Zeppelin reunion, Bowie, Madonna, Dire Straits, the Who, Elton John, Black Sabbath, and dozens more. No later hits, no filler — just the music of the day itself. At 100 songs across eight 50-song games and two 75-song Marathons, it's a double pack — 2,000 cards and ten playlists, twice the size of a standard edition.

$5 from every purchase goes to the Souls Harbour Rescue Mission in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada — about one hot meal for a neighbour in need. Donations are made at the end of each month, based on that month's sales. Feeding people was the whole point in 1985; on this pack, it still is.
