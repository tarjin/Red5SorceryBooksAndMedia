About the Press

Small, Well-Made Things

Red5Sorcery Books & Media is a boutique press from Halifax, Nova Scotia, built around the idea that small, well-made things still matter. Art, science, and a boutique shop — Red5Sorcery makes things for safety, play & imagination.

The Maker

Founder · author · conductor

Tarjin Rahman

Tarjin Rahman lives in Halifax. He writes the books, draws the cats, and runs Red5Sorcery Books & Media — a boutique press built around the idea that small, well-made things still matter. Before this, he worked at Microsoft, Adobe, and various other tech companies and adventures. After decades in industry, his passion lives here.

How the Work Is Made

Human taste · modern tools

The press runs the modern way: a human author directing a small council of AI collaborators. The tools bring speed and range; the taste, the judgment, and the final call stay human. It's how one person can keep a real catalogue moving — books, games, gifts, and reference works — without thinning any of it out.

The same principle runs through everything here, whether it's a cartoon, a bingo pack, a steganography manual, or a jingle for a local business: use the best methods available, and never let the method outrank the work.
